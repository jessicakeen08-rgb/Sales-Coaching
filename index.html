How it works

A focused block of coaching, long enough to change how you work and short enough to keep momentum.

  1. 01

    Free intro call

    In a 30 minute call we talk through where you are, what you want to change and what success looks like. If we are a good fit, we agree what to focus on.

  2. 02

    Six coaching sessions

    Six sessions of 60 minutes, built around your live deals, calls and team. You share a short update beforehand so every session starts in the right place.

  3. 03

    Commitments between sessions

    Each session ends with a few clear actions. We review them next time, so the coaching shows up in your results and not only in your notes.
